Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2025

This policy explains how ArcadeHub (darkrealmgames.co.uk) collects, uses and protects any information you provide when you use this website.

1. Who We Are

ArcadeHub is a free online games portal available at darkrealmgames.co.uk. If you have any questions, please contact us at tavibpm@gmail.com.

2. What Information We Collect

ArcadeHub is designed to be used without an account. We do not require registration or personal details to play games. We may collect the following automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Approximate location (country or city level only)
  • Referral source (how you arrived at the site)

This information is collected in aggregate and is never used to identify you personally.

3. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience:

  • Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the site.
  • Advertising cookies are used by Google AdSense to show relevant ads based on your interests.
  • Functional cookies remember your preferences such as the last game category you browsed.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. For more on Google's advertising data use, visit policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdSense for advertisements. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics for understanding site traffic and usage.
  • Google Fonts for typography, served via Google CDN.

5. Children's Privacy

ArcadeHub is designed for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 and we never require registration to play. Parents or guardians with concerns are welcome to contact us at tavibpm@gmail.com.

6. How We Use Your Information

  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it
  • To display relevant advertising that keeps the site free
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues

We do not sell, trade or rent personal data to third parties.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to know what data is held about you, request its correction or deletion, and opt out of personalised advertising via your browser or Google ad settings. Contact us at tavibpm@gmail.com to exercise any of these rights.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
