Built by a family, played by everyone

ArcadeHub started as a project to give David, Maya and Andy a safe, clean place to play great browser games without ads that interrupt, accounts they have to create or downloads they have to trust.

It turned into something we thought other families might enjoy too. So here it is: free, open, and always a work in progress.
