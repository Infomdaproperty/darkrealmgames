About ArcadeHub

Free browser games, zero friction. No download, no login, no fuss. Just play.

✨ Our Story

ArcadeHub started with a simple idea: give kids a safe, clean place to play great games online without accounts to remember, apps to install, or pop-ups chasing them around.

Built by a parent, tested by three very demanding children, and refined until it was fast and fun on every device, including a battered Android phone with a cracked screen.

Every game on this site loads in seconds, works on mobile and desktop, and is completely free. No hidden paywalls. No dark patterns. Just games.

📋 Our Mission

The internet is full of game sites buried under pop-ups, autoplay videos and login walls. ArcadeHub is the opposite: a clean, fast, distraction-light place where the game is the point.

We add new games regularly, keep the experience smooth across all devices, and stay free, always.
